1. Warm-up (暖身閒聊)

1. 以前 / 以後 / 剛剛
💡 Explanation: "剛剛" (gāng gāng) can only be used for things that just happened. "以前" (yǐ qián) placed at the beginning of a sentence means "in the past"; when used to express "before doing something", it is placed after the verb. "以後" (yǐ hòu) works the same way for "after doing something". When referring to "when doing something", "以" can be omitted.
我(ㄨㄛˇ)剛(ㄍㄤ)剛(ㄍㄤ)起(ㄑㄧˇ)床(ㄔㄨㄤˊ)。
I just woke up.
正(ㄓㄥˋ)常(ㄔㄤˊ)我(ㄨㄛˇ)上(ㄕㄤˋ)中(ㄓㄨㄥ)文(ㄨㄣˊ)課(ㄎㄜˋ)的(ㄉㄜ˙)時(ㄕˊ)候(ㄏㄡˋ)，也(ㄧㄝˇ)是(ㄕˋ)上(ㄕㄤˋ)課(ㄎㄜˋ)(以(ㄧˇ))前(ㄑㄧㄢˊ)才(ㄘㄞˊ)起(ㄑㄧˇ)床(ㄔㄨㄤˊ)。
Normally when I take Chinese class, I don't get up until right before class.
*Note: Here "才" (cái) indicates that the speaker feels an action happened late, slowly, or later than expected.

6. 知道 (zhī dào) vs. 會 (huì)
💡 Explanation: "知道" means knowing or understanding a fact/information. "會" means knowing how to do something or being skilled at it. "不太會" is commonly used to express not being very good at something.
那(ㄋㄚˋ)個(ㄍㄜ˙)孩(ㄏㄞˊ)子(ㄗ˙)去(ㄑㄩˋ)年(ㄋㄧㄢˊ)不(ㄅㄨˊ)太(ㄊㄞˋ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)說(ㄕㄨㄛ)話(ㄏㄨㄚˋ)，今(ㄐㄧㄣ)年(ㄋㄧㄢˊ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)說(ㄕㄨㄛ)很(ㄏㄣˇ)多(ㄉㄨㄛ)英(ㄧㄥ)文(ㄨㄣˊ)單(ㄉㄢ)字(ㄗˋ)和(ㄏㄜˋ)句(ㄐㄩˋ)子(ㄗ˙)。
That child couldn't speak much last year, but this year can speak many English vocabulary words and sentences.
*Note: In English, people say "words" (單字), but in Chinese, people don't usually say "單字" for Chinese words because a single character (字) can be a word, or two or three characters together can form a word. So for Chinese, people just say "說中文" directly, rather than "說中文單字".

3. Lesson 12 (第十二課: 幾 vs 多)

💡 Overview: Comparing the differences in usage and feeling between "幾" (jǐ) and "多" (duō).

Tone & Feeling Limiting / Sparse Expansive / Additive
Meaning A few / several (small amount) More than a base number / multiple
Applicable Base Numbers Strictly single digits (1–9, always less than 10) Must be base numbers 10 and above (10, 100, 1000) or divisible measure units (months, kg, dollars)
Example 幾個 (1–9)

五百多個 (500–5xx)

1. 幾 (jǐ - A few / several)
💡 Explanation: Expresses a very small quantity (strictly 1–9, always less than 10).
老(ㄌㄠˇ)婆(ㄆㄛˊ)：我(ㄨㄛˇ)們(ㄇㄣ˙)家(ㄐㄧㄚ)有(ㄧㄡˇ)很(ㄏㄣˇ)多(ㄉㄨㄛ)蘋(ㄆㄧㄥˊ)果(ㄍㄨㄛˇ)嗎(ㄇㄚ˙)？老(ㄌㄠˇ)公(ㄍㄨㄥ)：沒(ㄇㄟˊ)有(ㄧㄡˇ)，只(ㄓˇ)有(ㄧㄡˇ)幾(ㄐㄧˇ)個(ㄍㄜ˙)！
Wife: Do we have many apples at home? Husband: No, only a few!
2. Simple Formulas & Explanations for 多 and 幾
💡 Simple Formulas:
1. Start with a base number, e.g., 15. Replace the single digit "5" with "幾" (jǐ) to get "十幾" (teens / 10-something).
2. For 3,500, replace "500" with "多" (duō) to get "三千多" (over 3,000 / 3,000+).
3. For 4.8 km, use "多" to get "四個多公里" (a little over 4 km).
4. For 1.5 months, use "多" to get "一個多月" (a little over a month). Notice "個" goes between the number and "多".
⚠️ Important Note: You cannot say "四個多人" because people cannot be split into fractions (0.x). To say "more than 4 people", say "四個人以上" (4 people or more).
💬 Practical Sentences & Dialogues
