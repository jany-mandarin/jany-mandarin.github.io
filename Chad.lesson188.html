1. Warm-up (暖身閒聊)

1. 沒有 vs 不會
沒有 (méi yǒu): Used for actions that did not happen in the past.
不會 (bú huì): Used for future or present situations (won't happen / cannot do).
你(ㄋㄧˇ)現(ㄒㄧㄢˋ)在(ㄗㄞˋ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)背(ㄅㄟˋ)痛(ㄊㄨㄥˋ)嗎(ㄇㄚ˙)？我(ㄨㄛˇ)現(ㄒㄧㄢˋ)在(ㄗㄞˋ)不(ㄅㄨˊ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)痛(ㄊㄨㄥˋ)。

8. 一直 vs 一定
一直 (yì zhí): has an "eye" (目) underneath -> continuously looking at / doing something.
一定 (yí dìng): has "walk" (走-like) underneath -> will definitely execute something.
*Note: "一定沒問題" is used to give someone confidence (You'll definitely be fine).
