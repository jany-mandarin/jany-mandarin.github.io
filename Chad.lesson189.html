2. 簡體中文 / 繁體中文 (Simplified vs. Traditional Chinese)
【 簡 (jiǎn) 】
你(ㄋㄧˇ)看(ㄎㄢˋ)得(ㄉㄜ˙)懂(ㄉㄨㄥˇ)簡(ㄐㄧㄢˇ)體(ㄊㄧˇ)中(ㄓㄨㄥ)文(ㄨㄣˊ)嗎(ㄇㄚ˙)？我(ㄨㄛˇ)看(ㄎㄢˋ)得(ㄉㄜ˙)懂(ㄉㄨㄥˇ)，可(ㄎㄜˇ)是(ㄕˋ)我(ㄨㄛˇ)不(ㄅㄨˋ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)寫(ㄒㄧㄝˇ)。

💡 Character Origin Note: In ancient China, bamboo was very easy to obtain, so the top part of "簡" contains bamboo (竹). Its pronunciation is also similar to "間" (jiān).
【 繁 (fán) 】
你(ㄋㄧˇ)想(ㄒㄧㄤˇ)學(ㄒㄩㄝˊ)簡(ㄐㄧㄢˇ)體(ㄊㄧˇ)中(ㄓㄨㄥ)文(ㄨㄣˊ)嗎(ㄇㄚ˙)？我(ㄨㄛˇ)...不(ㄅㄨˋ)想(ㄒㄧㄤˇ)學(ㄒㄩㄝˊ)，因(ㄧㄣ)為(ㄨㄟˋ)我(ㄨㄛˇ)不(ㄅㄨˋ)想(ㄒㄧㄤˇ)去(ㄑㄩˋ)中(ㄓㄨㄥ)國(ㄍㄨㄛˊ)，我(ㄨㄛˇ)只(ㄓˇ)想(ㄒㄧㄤˇ)去(ㄑㄩˋ)台(ㄊㄞˊ)灣(ㄨㄢ)。
Do you want to learn Simplified Chinese? I... don't want to learn it, because I don't want to go to China, I only want to go to Taiwan.
💡 Character Origin Note: "繁" means complicated or performing intricate tasks repeatedly. It contains "每" (every), a hand (手) on the top right, and "糸" (silk/thread) at the bottom. Imagine in ancient times, making clothes required many complex steps, which is why Traditional Chinese characters are called "繁體字".
【 體 (tǐ) 】
💡 Character Origin Note: The original meaning of "體" is body. The left side is "骨" (bone), because the body is supported by bones. The right side comes from "禮" (ritual/gift), representing items prepared for rituals. You can imagine the body as an instrument for offering rituals.

3. Lesson 11 (第十一課: 就 vs 才)

Compare 1: Time (時間) — Subject + Time + 就 / 才 + Verb
就 (Early / Fast / Easy)
我(ㄨㄛˇ)常(ㄔㄤˊ)常(ㄔㄤˊ)很(ㄏㄣˇ)早(ㄗㄠˇ)就(ㄐㄧㄡˋ)去(ㄑㄩˋ)睡(ㄕㄨㄟˋ)覺(ㄐㄧㄠˋ)了(ㄌㄜ˙)。

才 (Late / Slow / Hard)

💡 Explanation: Use 就 when the speaker feels an action happened early or quickly. Use 才 when the speaker feels it happened late or slowly.
