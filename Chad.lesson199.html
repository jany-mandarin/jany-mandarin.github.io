一(ㄧ)、暖(ㄋㄨㄢˇ)身(ㄕㄣ) (Warm-up)

1. 中(ㄓㄨㄥ)華(ㄏㄨㄚˊ)航(ㄏㄤˊ)空(ㄎㄨㄥ) (China Airlines)
Explanation: 航 (háng) can be a verb or a noun. In ancient times, it meant a boat moving forward: the left side "舟" means a boat, and the right side "亢" shows a person standing and moving forward. Today, it refers to traveling by boat, plane, or spaceship.

Last time I took a China Airlines flight, I ordered a glass of boba milk tea.

Note: 少 in 空少 is pronounced with 4th tone (ㄕㄠˋ, shào), which is used for young male job titles. Do NOT confuse it with 3rd tone (ㄕㄠˇ, shǎo), which means "less" or "missing".

1. 畫(ㄏㄨㄚˋ)畫(ㄏㄨㄚˋ) (To draw / Painting)
Explanation: The character 畫 (huà) has two meanings: a verb (to draw/paint) and a noun (a painting or picture). Therefore, in 畫畫, the first 畫 is the verb "to draw", and the second 畫 is the noun "picture".
