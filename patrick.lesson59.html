一、第十課 (Lesson 10)

1. 不要...了 / 別...了
Explanation: 「不要」 (bùyào) when spoken quickly becomes 「别」 (bié). It is used to stop someone from doing something or tell someone not to do an action.
别bié别玩wán玩手shǒu手机jī机了le了！
Stop playing on your phone!
别bié别睡shuì睡觉jiào觉了le了！
Stop sleeping! / Wake up!
别bié别看kàn看电diàn电视shì视了le了！
Stop watching TV!
别bié别看kàn看书shū书了le了！
Stop reading books!
2. A 对 B + adj.
Explanation: Shows how A treats B (one-way attitude or treatment). If you use 「和」 (hé), it means a mutual, two-way relationship.
跑步对身体很好。
Running is very good for health.
老师对学生非常好。
The teacher is extremely good to the students.
我的公司对我不好。
My company is not good to me.
我对我的女友非常好。
I am very good to my girlfriend.
3. 正在 (zhèngzài)
Explanation: Indicates that an action is currently happening right at this moment.
我正在上中文课。
I am having a Chinese class right now.
哥哥正在玩电脑，没时间帮助我。
Older brother is playing on the computer right now, so he has no time to help me.
4. 洗 (xǐ)
Explanation: Broadly used for washing or cleaning something with water.
那件白色的衣服我帮你洗了，在外面呢。
I helped you wash that white piece of clothing; it's outside.

二、第十一課 (Lesson 11)

1. S. + V. + 的 + N.
Explanation: In Chinese, descriptive phrases or relative clauses are placed before the noun they modify, whereas in English they usually go after.
這是我新买的衣服。
This is the clothes I newly bought.
這是我妈妈做的饭。
This is the meal my mom made.
和你一起看电视的人是谁？
Who is the person watching TV together with you?
這是我昨天买的炒饭。
This is the fried rice I bought yesterday.
這是我做的海南鸡饭。
This is the Hainanese chicken rice I made.
這是我女友买的杯子。
This is the cup my girlfriend bought.
2. A + 比 + B + adj.
Explanation: Comparative structure meaning A is more [adj.] than B.
我比哥哥高。
I am taller than older brother.
今天比昨天热。
Today is hotter than yesterday.
今天的西瓜比昨天便宜。
Today's watermelon is cheaper than yesterday's.
3. A + 没有 + B + adj.
Explanation: Negative comparison meaning A is not as [adj.] as B.
哥哥没有姐姐高。
Older brother is not as tall as older sister.
今天没有昨天热。
Today is not as hot as yesterday.
西瓜没有苹果便宜。
Watermelon is not as cheap as apple.
4. A + 比 + B + adj. + num-M
Explanation: Comparative structure with a specific measurement or degree difference.
西瓜比苹果贵两块钱。
Watermelon is 2 yuan more expensive than apple.
我的学习比他好一点。
My studies are a bit better than his.
今天比昨天热得多。
Today is much hotter than yesterday.
他比我们老师小两岁。
He is 2 years younger than our teacher.
我哥比我大两岁。
My older brother is 2 years older than me.
5. 可能 (kěnéng)
Explanation: Expresses probability or possibility (maybe / probably).
明天可能会下雨。
It might rain tomorrow.
他可能知道这件事情了。
He probably already knows about this matter.
可能我明天不来上班了。
I might not come to work tomorrow.
你可能不认识他。
You probably don't know him.
