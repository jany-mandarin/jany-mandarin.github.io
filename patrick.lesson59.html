一、第十課

1. 不要...了 / 別...了

Explanation: 不要就是這兩個字念快一點就會變成別了，意思是制止某人做某事，或是不要讓某人做某事
别(bié)玩(wán)手(shǒu)机(jī)了(le)！Stop playing on your phone!
别(bié)睡(shuì)觉(jiào)了(le)！Stop sleeping!
别(bié)看(kàn)电(diàn)视(shì)了(le)！Stop watching TV!
别(bié)看(kàn)书(shū)了(le)！Stop reading books!

2. A對B+adj.

Explanation: 表示a怎麼對待b，這是單向的，如果這裡是放和，就是雙向的
跑(pǎo)步(bù)对(duì)身(shēn)体(tǐ)很(hěn)好(hǎo)。Running is very good for health.
老(lǎo)师(shī)对(duì)学(xué)生(shēng)非(fēi)常(cháng)好(hǎo)。The teacher is extremely good to the students.
我(wǒ)的(de)公(gōng)司(sī)對(duì)我(wǒ)不(bù)好(hǎo)。My company is not good to me.
我(wǒ)對(duì)我(wǒ)的(de)女(nǚ)友(yǒu)非(fēi)常(cháng)好(hǎo)。I am very good to my girlfriend.

3. 正在

Explanation: 表示某個人現在此時此刻正在做某件事情
我(wǒ)正(zhèng)在(zài)上(shàng)中(zhōng)文(wén)課(kè)。I am having a Chinese class right now.
哥(gē)哥(ge)正(zhèng)在(zài)玩(wán)電(diàn)腦(nǎo)，沒(méi)時(shí)間(jiān)幫(bāng)助(zhù)我(wǒ)。Older brother is playing on the computer, no time to help me.

4. 洗

Explanation: 廣泛用在用水清洗了某個東西
那(nà)件(jiàn)白(bái)色(sè)的(de)衣(yī)服(fu)我(wǒ)帮(bāng)你(nǐ)洗(xǐ)了(le)，在(zài)外(wài)面(miàn)呢(ne)。I helped you wash that white clothing, it's outside.

二、第十一課

1. S.+V.+的+N.

Explanation: 中文習慣把解釋的東西放在名詞前面，英文則是放在名詞的後面
這(zhè)是(shì)我(wǒ)新(xīn)买(mǎi)的(de)衣(yī)服(fu)。This is the clothes I newly bought.
這(zhè)是(shì)我(wǒ)妈(mā)妈(ma)做(zuò)的(de)饭(fàn)。This is the meal my mom made.
和(hé)你(nǐ)一(yì)起(qǐ)看(kàn)电(diàn)视(shì)的(de)人(rén)是(shì)誰(shuí)？Who is the person watching TV with you?
這(zhè)是(shì)我(wǒ)昨(zuó)天(tiān)買(mǎi)的(de)炒(chǎo)飯(fàn)。This is the fried rice I bought yesterday.
這(zhè)是(shì)我(wǒ)做(zuò)的(de)海(hǎi)南(nán)雞(jī)飯(fàn)。This is the Hainanese chicken rice I made.
這(zhè)是(shì)我(wǒ)女友(nǚyǒu)買(mǎi)的(de)杯(bēi)子(zi)。This is the cup my girlfriend bought.

2. A+比+B+adj.

我(wǒ)比(bǐ)哥(gē)哥(ge)高(gāo)I am taller than older brother
今(jīn)天(tiān)比(bǐ)昨(zuó)天(tiān)热(rè)Today is hotter than yesterday
今(jīn)天(tiān)的(de)西(xī)瓜(guā)比(bǐ)昨(zuó)天(tiān)便(biàn)宜(yi)。Today's watermelon is cheaper than yesterday's.

3. A+沒有+B+adj.

哥(gē)哥(ge)沒(méi)有(yǒu)姊(jiě)姊(jie)高(gāo)。Older brother is not as tall as older sister.
今(jīn)天(tiān)沒(méi)有(yǒu)昨(zuó)天(tiān)熱(rè)。Today is not as hot as yesterday.
西(xī)瓜(guā)沒(méi)有(yǒu)蘋(píng)果(guǒ)便(biàn)宜(yi)。Watermelon is not as cheap as apple.

4. A+比+B.+adj.+num-M

西(xī)瓜(guā)比(bǐ)苹(píng)果(guǒ)贵(guì)两(liǎng)块(kuài)钱(qián)。Watermelon is 2 yuan more expensive than apple.
我(wǒ)的(de)學(xué)習(xí)比(bǐ)他(tā)好(hǎo)一(yì)点(diǎn)。My study is a bit better than his.
今(jīn)天(tiān)比(bǐ)昨(zuó)天(tiān)热(rè)得(de)多(duō)。Today is much hotter than yesterday.
他(tā)比(bǐ)我(wǒ)們(men)老(lǎo)師(shī)小(xiǎo)兩(liǎng)岁(suì)。He is 2 years younger than our teacher.
我(wǒ)哥(gē)比(bǐ)我(wǒ)大(dà)兩(liǎng)岁(suì)。My older brother is 2 years older than me.

5. 可能

明(míng)天(tiān)可(kě)能(néng)會(huì)下(xià)雨(yǔ)。It might rain tomorrow.
他(tā)可(kě)能(néng)知(zhī)道(dào)這(zhè)件(jiàn)事(shì)情(qing)了(le)。He probably knows about this matter.
可(kě)能(néng)我(wǒ)明(míng)天(tiān)不(bù)來(lái)上(shàng)班(bān)了(le)。I might not come to work tomorrow.
你(nǐ)可(kě)能(néng)不(bù)認(rèn)識(shi)他(tā)。You probably don't know him.
