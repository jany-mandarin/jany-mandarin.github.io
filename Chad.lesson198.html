1. 罰 (fá - to punish / fine)

Explanation: You can imagine in ancient times when police caught bad guys, they used a net (𦉴/罒) to capture them, used their mouth (言) to question what bad things they did, and used a knife (刂) to punish them. Therefore, this character means to punish or fine.

  • 罰(ㄈㄚˊ)寫(ㄒㄧㄝˇ) — write lines as punishment
  • 罰(ㄈㄚˊ)錢(ㄑㄧㄢˊ) — pay a fine

5. A在B旁邊 = A旁邊有B

Explanation:
"A 在 B 旁邊" (A is next to B) communicates location.
"B 旁邊有 A" (Next to B, there is A) communicates existence at a place.

4. 去睡覺 (Go to sleep / Head to bed) vs 睡覺 (Sleep)

Grammar Nuance:
去睡覺: Emphasizes the physical motion or action of heading off to bed.
睡覺: Simply refers to the state or act of sleeping itself.

Lesson 12

Structure: Subject + Verb + (time span) + Object

Grammar Structure: In Chinese, when emphasizing how long an action has been performed, the time span (duration) is placed directly between the Verb and the Object.

💡 Review Note: For the distinction between 就 (jiù - earlier than expected) and 才 (cái - later than expected), you can review Lesson 188 Grammar Notes.
