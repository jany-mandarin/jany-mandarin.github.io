1. Warm-up (暖身閒聊)

1. 日期的前後 (Time Expressions: Past, Present & Future)
💡 Explanation: In Chinese, time expressions use 「前/上」 for the past, 「這/今」 for the present, and 「後/下/明」 for the future. Look at the chart below for comparison:
以前 (Before / Past) 現在 (Present / Now) 以後 (After / Future)
去年 (qù nián - last year) 今年 (jīn nián - this year) 明年 (míng nián - next year)
上個月 (shàng gè yuè - last month) 這個月 (zhè gè yuè - this month) 下個月 (xià gè yuè - next month)
上個禮拜 (shàng gè lǐ bài - last week) 這個禮拜 (zhè gè lǐ bài - this week) 下個禮拜 (xià gè lǐ bài - next week)
昨天 (zuó tiān - yesterday) 今天 (jīn tiān - today) 明天 (míng tiān - tomorrow)
上個小時 (shàng gè xiǎo shí - an hour ago) 這個小時 (zhè gè xiǎo shí - this hour) 下個小時 (xià gè xiǎo shí - next hour)
上一分鐘 (shàng yì fēn zhōng - a minute ago) 這一分鐘 (zhè yì fēn zhōng - this minute) 下一分鐘 (xià yì fēn zhōng - next minute)
上次 (shàng cì - last time) 這次 (zhè cì - this time) 下次 (xià cì - next time)

4. 寫 (xiě - Write) vs 說 (shuō - Speak / Tell)
💡 Explanation:
• 寫 (xiě) is used for physical writing on paper, menus, books, or writing formal emails.
• For messaging on instant messaging apps (like LINE or WhatsApp), native speakers prefer saying 「用 LINE 跟我說」 (tell me on LINE) rather than 「寫在 LINE」 because text messaging is treated like casual oral communication.
要(ㄧㄠˋ)是(ㄕˋ)我(ㄨㄛˇ)去(ㄑㄩˋ)臺(ㄊㄞˊ)灣(ㄨㄢ)，我(ㄨㄛˇ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)用(ㄩㄥˋ) LINE 跟(ㄍㄣ)你(ㄋㄧˇ)說(ㄕㄨㄛ)。
If I go to Taiwan, I will message you on LINE.
這(ㄓㄜˋ)本(ㄅㄣˇ)書(ㄕㄨ)上(ㄕㄤˋ)寫(ㄒㄧㄝˇ)得(ㄉㄜ˙)很(ㄏㄣˇ)有(ㄧㄡˇ)意(ㄧˋ)思(ㄙ˙)。

2. Grammar Comparison: A 跟 B vs A 對 B
💡 Explanation: Notice the difference in direction! 「跟/和」 is a two-way connection (A 🤝 B), while 「對」 is a one-way arrow (A ➔ B).
Pattern A 和 / 跟 B A 對 B
Meaning You and I together, or action WITH you (English: with / and). My attitude, feeling, or action TOWARDS you (English: towards / to).
Direction Two-way / Mutual (A 🤝 B)
Connects both parties doing an action together or having a mutual relationship.		 One-way Arrow (A ➔ B)
Originates from A, pointing towards B's direction (attitude, feeling, trait).
Structure A + 跟 + B + (一起) + Action
or A + 跟 + B + 一樣		 A + 對 + B + Attitude / Feeling / One-way action
Examples for 「A 和/跟 B」 (Two-way):
我(ㄨㄛˇ)跟(ㄍㄣ)太(ㄊㄞˋ)太(tai)一(ㄧˋ)起(ㄑㄧˇ)去(ㄑㄩˋ)看(ㄎㄢˋ)電(ㄉㄧㄢˋ)影(ㄧㄥˇ)。
I go to watch a movie together with my wife.
我(ㄨㄛˇ)跟(ㄍㄣ)你(ㄋㄧˇ)說(ㄕㄨㄛ)：你(ㄋㄧˇ)今(ㄐㄧㄣ)天(ㄊㄧㄢ)很(ㄏㄣˇ)帥(ㄕㄨㄞˋ)。
I tell you: You look very handsome today.
Examples for 「A 對 B」 (One-way):
我(ㄨㄛˇ)對(ㄉㄨㄟˋ)牛(ㄋㄧㄡˊ)奶(ㄋㄞˇ)過(ㄍㄨㄛˋ)敏(ㄇㄧㄣˇ)。

💡 Explanation:
• 旅行 (lǚ xíng) refers to traveling somewhere for several days, weeks, or months.
• 小旅行 (xiǎo lǚ xíng) refers to a short trip or weekend getaway lasting only 1 or 2 days.
