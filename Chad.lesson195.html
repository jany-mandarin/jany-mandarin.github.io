💡 Quick Hint:
• At start of sentence: 「以前」 (In the past) / 「以後」 (In the future)
• After time or action: [Time / Action] + 前 / 以前 / 後 / 以後
1. "Three days ago, I bought a book."
2. "In the future, I want to live in Taipei."
3. "After eating meals, I will wash the dishes."
4. "Two years ago, I started learning Chinese."
5. "In the past, I had never been to Korea; next year I will go to Korea."
