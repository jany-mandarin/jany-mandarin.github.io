3. 禮(ㄌㄧˇ)貌(ㄇㄠˋ)用(ㄩㄥˋ)語(ㄩˇ) (Polite / Indirect Language)

Explanation: Saying "不好吃" (not tasty) or "我不喜歡" (I don't like it) directly is not very polite. So people often say something is "有點鹹" (a little salty) or "太辣了" (too spicy) to hint that they don't really like it, without saying it directly.

4. 說(ㄕㄨㄛ)話(ㄏㄨㄚˋ)很(ㄏㄣˇ)像(ㄒㄧㄤˋ)小(ㄒㄧㄠˇ)朋(ㄆㄥˊ)友(ㄧㄡˇ) (Talks like a kid)

Translation Practice

Next week I'm going to Taiwan on a business trip, so these past few days I've been busy every day.

Today my company didn't have many meetings, so I only went into work for a little bit, and then I booked a room. I booked Taipei Era Residence, because it's very close to Taipei Main Station, so it's convenient to go anywhere. There's also a washing machine there, and it's free, so I can pack fewer clothes.

In the evening I put things into my suitcase, and I packed a pair of waterproof shoes, because I heard it often rains in Taiwan. I also realized that there happens to be a holiday next week, so after the business trip, I can stay in Taiwan a few more days to have fun — I'm really happy about that.

Tomorrow at 10am, I'll board the plane. These past few days I've always been looking forward to this trip. At first I couldn't find a map, and I was a little nervous, but then I found out there's already a map on my phone, so I felt relieved.

Tomorrow, this trip is finally going to begin.
