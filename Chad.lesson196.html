1. Warm-up

1. 區分「來」和「去」 (Distinguishing 「來」 and 「去」)
💡 Rules & Perspective:
• 來 (to come): Used when someone/something is moving closer to the speaker's current location.
• 去 (to go): Used when someone/something is moving away from the speaker's current location toward another place.
昨(ㄗㄨㄛˊ)天(ㄊㄧㄢ)有(ㄧㄡˇ)很(ㄏㄣˇ)多(ㄉㄨㄛ)小(ㄒㄧㄠˇ)朋(ㄆㄥˊ)友(ㄧㄡˇ)來(ㄌㄞˊ)我(ㄨㄛˇ)們(ㄇㄣ˙)家(ㄐㄧㄚ)拿(ㄋㄚˊ)糖(ㄊㄤˊ)果(ㄍㄨㄛˇ)。
Yesterday many children came to our house to get candy.

I am going to Taiwan tomorrow.

💡 Explanation: Formal written Chinese (書面語) uses precise words found in books, formal documents, and news. Spoken Chinese (口語) uses simpler, everyday words evolved for ease of speech.

6. 玩 + 東西 或 不加東西 (Play / Hang out)
