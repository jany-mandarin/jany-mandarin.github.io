1. 發(ㄈㄚ)現(ㄒㄧㄢˋ) / 發(ㄈㄚ)現(ㄒㄧㄢˋ)了(ㄌㄜ˙) (Notice / Discover)

發(ㄈㄚ)現(ㄒㄧㄢˋ)
發(ㄈㄚ)現(ㄒㄧㄢˋ)了(ㄌㄜ˙)
English Like "notice" — it simply introduces a new piece of information. The listener's attention jumps straight to the fact itself. Like "discover" / "I figured it out!" / "I found it" — used when you finally caught or found something after looking or observing, often something surprising (a secret, or something you never knew before).
Explanation Because the focus isn't on the action of discovering itself — it's just on the new fact — you don't add 「了」. This shows that after looking or observing, you finally caught / found something, and you feel surprised about it.
Pattern 「發現」+ [A Clause / Situation] (Without 「了」) 「發現了」+ [A Specific Noun] (With 「了」) / Used alone as an exclamation

3. 旅(ㄌㄩˇ)行(ㄒㄧㄥˊ) / 玩(ㄨㄢˊ) (Travel / Play & have fun)

Explanation: 旅行 means "to travel" — it describes the whole trip in general. 玩 means "to play / have fun" — it describes what you actually do once you get to a specific place. So you usually use 旅行 for the trip as a whole, and 玩 for enjoying time at one particular spot inside that trip.

4. 九(ㄐㄧㄡˇ)份(ㄈㄣˋ) (Jiufen)

Explanation: Jiufen (九份) is in the mountains. Long ago, very few families lived there — only nine households (九戶人家). Whenever people went out to buy goods, or ship supplies in by sea, for convenience they would always divide the goods into nine equal shares (份) to split among the nine households. That's how the name "九份" (Nine Shares) came to be.

4. 內(ㄋㄟˋ)用(ㄩㄥˋ) (Dine in)

Explanation: 內 is made up of 入 (to enter) inside an outer shape. 入 means "to enter" — you'll often see it in 入口 (entrance). Since 內 contains 入 (to enter), it carries the sense of "going inward," which is why 內 means "inside."
