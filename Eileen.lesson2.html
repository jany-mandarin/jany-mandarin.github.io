Part 1: Hospital Dialogues (医院对话)

1. Hello there! What seems to be the problem today?

❌ 你好，你今天有问题吗？
Teacher's Note: "问题 (wèn tí)" means "question". You use it when you have a question to ask, not for a medical problem!
✅ 你nǐ好hǎo，请qǐng问wèn哪nǎ里lǐ不bù舒shū服fu？
Hello, where are you feeling uncomfortable?

2. I have a very bad stomachache

❌ 我有一个不好的胃痛
Teacher's Note: Patients usually say "肚子痛" (stomachache). "不好" means something is bad in quality. To describe illness, we say "很痛" (very painful) or "非常痛" (extremely painful).
✅ 我wǒ的de肚dù子zi很hěn痛tòng。

✅ 我wǒ的de胃wèi非fēi常cháng痛tòng。
My stomach is very painful. / My stomach is extremely painful.

3. I started a fever last night.

❌ 我昨晚有发烧。
Teacher's Note: The particle "了 (le)" here indicates a state change (from not having a fever to having one).
✅ 我wǒ昨zuó晚wǎn开kāi始shǐ发fā烧shāo了le！

4. How long have you had the fever?

❌ 你有发烧几天？
Teacher's Note: "有" implies a completed action in the past, but the fever is continuing today. Do not use "有".
✅ 你nǐ发fā烧shāo几jǐ天tiān了le？

5. Have you had any fever or vomiting?

Attention: 吐 here is 4th tone (tù).
你nǐ有yǒu没méi有yǒu发fā烧shāo还hái是shì吐tù？
Did you have a fever or vomit?

6. The fever has been for one day.

❌ 这个发烧一天
✅ 我wǒ发fā烧shāo一yì天tiān了le！
I have had a fever for one day!

7. I had diarrhea three times last night.

❌ 我拉肚子三时昨晚
✅ 我wǒ昨zuó晚wǎn拉lā肚dù子zi三sān次cì。

8. I vomited at the end.

✅ 然rán后hòu我wǒ吐tù了le。

9. Please open your mouth wide and stick out your tongue.

✅ 好hǎo，请qǐng张zhāng大dà嘴zuǐ巴bā，伸shēn出chū舌shé头tou，让ràng我wǒ看kàn看kan。

10. Please take deep breaths. Breathe in... Breathe out.

Teacher's Note: For normal actions like breathing out, "吐" is the 3rd tone (tǔ). For abnormal actions like vomiting, it is the 4th tone (tù).
✅ 请qǐng和hé我wǒ一yì起qǐ深shēn呼hū吸xī，吸xī气qì……吐tǔ气qì。

11. I'm going to touch your stomach. Does it hurt here?

✅ 我wǒ要yào摸mō一yí下xià你nǐ的de肚dù子zi，这zhè里lǐ会huì痛tòng吗ma？

12. Ways to describe stomach pain

Doctor:

你nǐ的de肚dù子zi是shì哪nǎ一yì种zhǒng痛tòng？
What kind of pain is in your stomach?

Patient Examples:

  • 刺cì痛tòng - Stabbing pain
  • 痉jìng挛luán - Cramping
  • 绞jiǎo痛tòng - Colic / Severe twisting pain
  • 像xiàng在zài烧shāo - Like it's burning

13. 刺 (cì) - to prick / stab

我wǒ的de手shǒu刺cì到dào了le。
My hand got pricked.

14. 麻醉 (má zuì) - Anesthesia

Teacher's Note: "麻 (má)" means numb. "醉 (zuì)" means drunk. You can imagine anesthesia is like making the patient fall down drunk!
我wǒ是shì麻má醉zuì医yī生shēng，可kě是shì我wǒ喜xǐ欢huan加jiā护hù病bìng房fáng医yī生shēng。
I am an anesthesiologist, but I like ICU doctors.

15. 加护病房 (jiā hù bìng fáng) - ICU

Teacher's Note: "加 (jiā)" is add. "护 (hù)" is protect. You can imagine patients in this room need "extra protection".
我wǒ喜xǐ欢huan在zài加jiā护hù病bìng房fáng做zuò医yī生shēng，可kě以yǐ和hé病bìng人rén说shuō话huà。
I like being a doctor in the ICU, I can talk to patients.

16. 安心 (ān xīn) - Feel at ease

我wǒ喜xǐ欢huan在zài加jiā护hù病bìng房fáng和hé病bìng人rén的de家jiā人rén说shuō话huà， 让ràng家jiā人rén安ān心xīn，我wǒ也yě有yǒu听tīng到dào他tā们men的de想xiǎng法fǎ。
I like talking to patients' families in the ICU to make them feel at ease, and I also hear their thoughts.

Part 2: Daily Dialogues (日常对话)

1. 常常 (cháng cháng) - Often

我wǒ今jīn天tiān没méi有yǒu去qù我wǒ常cháng常cháng去qù的de医yī院yuàn，
我wǒ不bù喜xǐ欢huan，因yīn为wèi我wǒ不bù知zhī道dào我wǒ要yào做zuò什shén么me。
I didn't go to the hospital I usually go to today. I don't like it because I don't know what I need to do.

2. 游泳池 (yóu yǒng chí) - Swimming pool

我wǒ上shàng星xīng期qī去qù游yóu泳yǒng池chí游yóu泳yǒng。
I went to the swimming pool to swim last week.

3. 岁 (suì) - Years old

我wǒ朋péng友you的de小xiǎo孩hái两liǎng岁suì。
My friend's child is two years old.

4 & 5. 牙套 (yá tào) - Braces & Months

Teacher's Note: "戴 (dài)" is used for wearing accessories, like wearing a hat (戴帽子) or a watch (戴手表).
Also, pay attention to "个" in months: 11个月 (11 months duration) vs 11月 (November).
我wǒ的de牙yá齿chǐ不bù舒shū服fu，所suǒ以yǐ我wǒ戴dài牙yá套tào。

你nǐ要yào戴dài牙yá套tào多duō久jiǔ？我wǒ要yào戴dài11shí yī个ge月yuè。
My teeth are uncomfortable, so I wear braces.
How long do you need to wear braces? I need to wear them for 11 months.

6. 很麻烦 (hěn má fan) - Very troublesome

我wǒ觉jué得de戴dài牙yá套tào很hěn麻má烦fan。
I think wearing braces is very troublesome.

7 & 8. 变 (biàn) & 训练 (xùn liàn) - Change & Train

Teacher's Note: "变 (biàn)" or "变成 (biàn chéng)" means to turn into or become.
我wǒ想xiǎng要yào我wǒ戴dài牙yá套tào后hòu变biàn舒shū服fu。

我wǒ在zài训xùn练liàn我wǒ的de狗gǒu变biàn好hǎo。

我wǒ的de老lǎo板bǎn训xùn练liàn我wǒ变biàn好hǎo。
I want to become comfortable after wearing braces.
I am training my dog to become good.
My boss trains me to become better.

9. 沟通 (gōu tōng) - Communication

Teacher's Note: "沟 (gōu)" means gap. "通 (tōng)" means keep clear/open. Together, it means bridging the gap to keep information flowing!
我wǒ下xià个ge月yuè要yào去qù澳ào大dà利lì亚yà上shàng沟gōu通tōng课kè，
帮bāng我wǒ和hé病bìng人rén能néng好hǎo好hǎo沟gōu通tōng。
I am going to Australia next month to take a communication class, to help me communicate well with patients.

10. 耐心 (nài xīn) - Patience

Teacher's Note: "耐 (nài)" means to endure. "心 (xīn)" means heart. Patience is having a heart that can endure.
我wǒ会huì对duì病bìng人rén很hěn有yǒu耐nài心xīn。
I will be very patient with the patients.
