1. Warm-up (暖身閒聊)

1. 豆漿 (dòu jiāng) / 豆奶 (dòu nǎi)
💡 Explanation: Taiwan has terms that are uniquely Taiwanese. "豆漿" (dòu jiāng) is very common in Taiwan, but outside Taiwan (such as in Hong Kong, Singapore, or Western countries), it is usually called "豆奶" (dòu nǎi). Similarly, Taiwan uses "捷運" (jié yùn), whereas outside Taiwan people say "地鐵" (dì tiě).
臺(ㄊㄞˊ)灣(ㄨㄢ)的(ㄉㄜ˙)早(ㄗㄠˇ)餐(ㄘㄢ)店(ㄉㄧㄢˋ)都(ㄉㄡ)有(ㄧㄡˇ)賣(ㄇㄞˋ)豆(ㄉㄡˋ)漿(ㄐㄧㄤ)，很(ㄏㄣˇ)多(ㄉㄨㄛ)臺(ㄊㄞˊ)灣(ㄨㄢ)人(ㄖㄣˊ)都(ㄉㄡ)喜(ㄒㄧˇ)歡(ㄏㄨㄢ)喝(ㄏㄜ)。
Breakfast shops in Taiwan all sell soy milk, and many Taiwanese people like to drink it.
2. 最喜歡 (zuì xǐ huān - Favorite)
💡 Explanation: "最" (zuì) means "most". "最喜歡" translates directly to "favorite" or "like the most".
我(ㄨㄛˇ)的(ㄉㄜ˙)貓(ㄇㄠ)是(ㄕˋ)我(ㄨㄛˇ)最(ㄗㄨㄟˋ)喜(ㄒㄧˇ)歡(ㄏㄨㄢ)的(ㄉㄜ˙)同(ㄊㄨㄥˊ)事(ㄕˋ)。
My cat is my favorite coworker.
3. 義大利麵 (yì dà lì miàn - Pasta / Spaghetti)
💡 Explanation: Although we previously learned "Italian style food" (義式料理), "義大利麵" is special because it is so common that people treat it as a specific everyday dish rather than just a general category.
我(ㄨㄛˇ)今(ㄐㄧㄣ)天(ㄊㄧㄢ)吃(ㄔ)白(ㄅㄞˊ)醬(ㄐㄧㄤˋ)義(ㄧˋ)大(ㄉㄚˋ)利(ㄌㄧˋ)麵(ㄇㄧㄢˋ)，我(ㄨㄛˇ)不(ㄅㄨˋ)喜(ㄒㄧˇ)歡(ㄏㄨㄢ)紅(ㄏㄨㄥˊ)醬(ㄐㄧㄤˋ)義(ㄧˋ)大(ㄉㄚˋ)利(ㄌㄧˋ)麵(ㄇㄧㄢˋ)。
Today I ate cream sauce (white sauce) pasta; I don't like tomato sauce (red sauce) pasta.
4. 過敏 (guò mǐn - Allergic)
💡 Explanation: Pattern: 對 sth. 過敏 (duì ... guò mǐn) = Allergic to something.
我(ㄨㄛˇ)對(ㄉㄨㄟˋ)牛(ㄋㄧㄡˊ)奶(ㄋㄞˇ)有(ㄧㄡˇ)一(ㄧˋ)點(ㄉㄧㄢˇ)過(ㄍㄨㄛˋ)敏(ㄇㄧㄣˇ)，我(ㄨㄛˇ)不(ㄅㄨˋ)能(ㄋㄥˊ)喝(ㄏㄜ)太(ㄊㄞˋ)多(ㄉㄨㄛ)牛(ㄋㄧㄡˊ)奶(ㄋㄞˇ)。
I am a little allergic to milk, so I cannot drink too much milk.
要(ㄧㄠˋ)是(ㄕˋ)我(ㄨㄛˇ)對(ㄉㄨㄟˋ)牛(ㄋㄧㄡˊ)奶(ㄋㄞˇ)過(ㄍㄨㄛˋ)敏(ㄇㄧㄣˇ)，我(ㄨㄛˇ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)很(ㄏㄣˇ)難(ㄋㄢˊ)過(ㄍㄨㄛˋ)。
If I were allergic to milk, I would be very sad.
*Note: Here "會" (huì) indicates a habitual inclination or a natural outcome ("would").

2. Homework Review & Extensions (上次作業與延伸)

1. What time is it? — It’s between 10:00 and 11:00 am.
❌ Incorrect: 現在幾點？早上十幾點。
✅ Correct:
現(ㄒㄧㄢˋ)在(ㄗㄞˋ)幾(ㄐㄧˇ)點(ㄉㄧㄢˋ)？現(ㄒㄧㄢˋ)在(ㄗㄞˋ)早(ㄗㄠˇ)上(ㄕㄤˋ)十(ㄕˊ)點(ㄉㄧㄢˋ)多(ㄉㄨㄛ)。
What time is it? It's past 10:00 am (between 10:00 and 11:00).
💡 Explanation: "幾" (jǐ) is used for small numbers (strictly 1–9). In "10:15 am", the minutes (15) are a larger number, so we use "多" (duō) rather than "幾".
2. How many customers came yesterday? — Forty-some.
❌ Incorrect: 昨天來了多少人？四十個人來了。
✅ Correct:
昨(ㄗㄨㄛˊ)天(ㄊㄧㄢ)來(ㄌㄞˊ)了(ㄌㄜ˙)多(ㄉㄨㄛ)少(ㄕㄠˇ)客(ㄎㄜˋ)人(ㄖㄣˊ)？四(ㄙˋ)十(ㄕˊ)幾(ㄐㄧˇ)位(ㄨㄟˋ)。
How many customers came yesterday? Forty-some customers.
💡 Explanation: "Forty-some" refers to a number between 41 and 49. Because 1–9 are single-digit small numbers, we use "幾" (jǐ) here (四十幾).
3. How many words have you memorized? — Two Hundreds-Three Hundreds
❌ Incorrect: 你知道很多字？我知道兩百多字。
✅ Correct:
你(ㄋㄧˇ)記(ㄐㄧˋ)得(ㄉㄜ˙)多(ㄉㄨㄛ)少(ㄕㄠˇ)(個(ㄍㄜ˙))字(ㄗˋ)？我(ㄨㄛˇ)記(ㄐㄧˋ)得(ㄉㄜ˙)兩(ㄌㄧㄤˇ)百(ㄅㄞˇ)多(ㄉㄨㄛ)個(ㄍㄜ˙)字(ㄗˋ)。
How many words do you remember? I remember over two hundred words.
💡 Explanation: We previously used "知道" (know) before learning "記得" (remember / memorize). For memorizing vocabulary, use "記得". The measure word "個" directly following "多少" can sometimes be omitted in spoken questions, but when placed after specific numbers like "兩百多", a measure word like "個" is required.

5. 語言 (yǔ yán - Language)
💡 Explanation: Refers to spoken/written languages. Measure word for language is "種" (zhǒng - type/kind).
你(ㄋㄧˇ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)說(ㄕㄨㄛ)幾(ㄐㄧˇ)種(ㄓㄨㄥˇ)語(ㄩˇ)言(ㄧㄢˊ)？我(ㄨㄛˇ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)說(ㄕㄨㄛ)三(ㄙㄢ)種(ㄓㄨㄥˇ)語(ㄩˇ)言(ㄧㄢˊ)。

6. 泰 (tài - Prosperous / Peaceful / Thai)
💡 Character Origin Note: Depicts a pair of large hands washing in a mountain stream, symbolizing abundance and prosperity. Most commonly used in "泰國" (Thailand).
我(ㄨㄛˇ)去(ㄑㄩˋ)過(ㄍㄨㄛˋ)泰(ㄊㄞˋ)國(ㄍㄨㄛˊ)。
I have been to Thailand.
7. 送 (sòng - Deliver / Escort / Give as a gift)
💡 Character Origin & Meanings: Originally depicted passing a torch outward. It carries three main usages: 1) Delivering objects, 2) Escorting/accompanying someone, 3) Giving a present for free.
他(ㄊㄚ)來(ㄌㄞˊ)做(ㄗㄨㄛˋ)什(ㄕㄣˊ)麼(ㄇㄜ˙)的(ㄉㄜ˙)？他(ㄊㄚ)來(ㄌㄞˊ)送(ㄙㄨㄥˋ)東(ㄉㄨㄥ)西(ㄒㄧ˙)給(ㄍㄟˇ)我(ㄨㄛˇ)的(ㄉㄜ˙)。

It's raining heavily now, you didn't bring an umbrella right? Let me walk/take you home.
8. 著 (zhe - Continuous State Particle)
💡 Explanation: Derived from holding bamboo over one's head. "著" indicates a continuous static state (like a still photograph), whereas "在" indicates a dynamic action taking place (like a video).
我(ㄨㄛˇ)穿(ㄔㄨㄢ)著(ㄓㄜ˙)衣(ㄧ)服(ㄈㄨˊ)。
I am wearing clothes.
*Note: Clothes remain continuously on the body, so "著" expresses this maintained status.
因(ㄧㄣ)為(ㄨㄟˋ)風(ㄈㄥ)很(ㄏㄣˇ)大(ㄉㄚˋ)，所(ㄙㄨㄛˇ)以(ㄧˇ)我(ㄨㄛˇ)用(ㄩㄥˋ)力(ㄌㄧˋ)抓(ㄓㄨㄚ)著(ㄓㄜ˙)雨(ㄩˇ)傘(ㄙㄢˇ)。
Because the wind was very strong, I firmly held onto the umbrella.
*Note: "著" shows a continuous holding action like a frozen picture frame.
說(ㄕㄨㄛ)著(ㄓㄜ˙)說(ㄕㄨㄛ)著(ㄓㄜ˙)，我(ㄨㄛˇ)就(ㄐㄧㄡˋ)哭(ㄎㄨ)了(ㄌㄜ˙)。
As I kept talking, I started crying.
*Note: Imagine someone continuously talking while a scene unfolds; this is very common in storybooks to enhance vividness.
9. 關係 (guān xì - Relationship / Connection)
💡 Explanation: "沒關係" has two meanings: 1) "No worries / It's fine." 2) "It has no relation to me, so there's no need to care." In spoken Chinese, "係" is sometimes dropped ("沒關"). (Review: Lesson 186)

Do you have a good relationship with your coworkers? I have a great relationship with my coworker, because my current coworker is my cat.
我(ㄨㄛˇ)跟(ㄍㄣ)小(ㄒㄧㄠˇ)明(ㄇㄧㄥˊ)的(ㄉㄜ˙)關(ㄍㄨㄢ)係(ㄒㄧˋ)不(ㄅㄨˋ)好(ㄏㄠˇ)，你(ㄋㄧˇ)不(ㄅㄨˊ)要(ㄧㄠˋ)跟(ㄍㄣ)我(ㄨㄛˇ)說(ㄕㄨㄛ)有(ㄧㄡˇ)關(ㄍㄨㄢ)小(ㄒㄧㄠˇ)明(ㄇㄧㄥˊ)的(ㄉㄜ˙)事(ㄕˋ)。

3. Lesson 12 (第十二課)

1. 國家 (guó jiā - Country / Nation)
地(ㄉㄧˋ)球(ㄑㄧㄡˊ)上(ㄕㄤˋ)最(ㄗㄨㄟˋ)大(ㄉㄚˋ)的(ㄉㄜ˙)國(ㄍㄨㄛˊ)家(ㄐㄧㄚ)是(ㄕˋ)哪(ㄋㄚˇ)一(ㄧˋ)個(ㄍㄜ˙)？是(ㄕˋ)俄(ㄜˋ)羅(ㄌㄨㄛˊ)斯(ㄙ)。

2. 旅行 (lǚ xíng - Travel)
💡 Character Origin Note: In ancient times, traveling required holding a flag so people could identify your country origin; the right side depicts multiple travelers. Commonly used as "去旅行" (go traveling).

3. 行 (xíng - Capable / Okay)
💡 Explanation: "行" can mean "capable / okay" when asking if someone can manage a task. However, "你行不行？" can sound slightly condescending or arrogant. In daily life, people prefer "可以" (kě yǐ) to express empathy and politeness.
你(ㄋㄧˇ)看(ㄎㄢˋ)起(ㄑㄧˇ)來(ㄌㄞˊ)很(ㄏㄣˇ)小(ㄒㄧㄠˇ)，這(ㄓㄜˋ)個(ㄍㄜ˙)很(ㄏㄣˇ)重(ㄓㄨㄥˋ)，你(ㄋㄧˇ)行(ㄒㄧㄥˊ)不(ㄅㄨˋ)行(ㄒㄧㄥˊ)？
You look small/young, and this is very heavy. Can you handle it?
*Polite Alternative: "這個很重，你可以嗎？小心。" (This is very heavy, are you okay? Be careful.)
4. 應該 (yīng gāi - Should / Expected to)
💡 Character Breakdown:
1. 應: Originally depicted an eagle near a cliff; adding "心" (heart) at the bottom represents a conviction inside your heart.
2. 該: Represents spoken words / what ought to be said.
現(ㄒㄧㄢˋ)在(ㄗㄞˋ)天(ㄊㄧㄢ)上(ㄕㄤˋ)很(ㄏㄟ)黑(ㄏㄟ)，等(ㄉㄥˇ)一(ㄧˋ)下(ㄒㄧㄚˋ)應(ㄧㄥ)該(ㄍㄞ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)下(ㄒㄧㄚˋ)雨(ㄩˇ)！
The sky is very dark now; it should rain in a bit!
*Note: "應該" carries a higher degree of probability than "可能". "可能" is uncertain, whereas "應該" is based on visible evidence.
你(ㄋㄧˇ)的(ㄉㄜ˙)貓(ㄇㄠ)生(ㄕㄥ)病(ㄅㄧㄥˋ)了(ㄌㄜ˙)，你(ㄋㄧˇ)應(ㄧㄥ)該(ㄍㄞ)要(ㄧㄠˋ)帶(ㄉㄞˋ)牠(ㄊㄚ)看(ㄎㄢˋ)醫(ㄧ)生(ㄕㄥ)。
Your cat is sick; you should take it to see a doctor.
