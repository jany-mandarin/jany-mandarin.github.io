1. Ways to Express "Leaving" (離開的說法)
💡 Explanation: Chinese has different verbs for leaving depending on whether you are leaving home, leaving the country, stepping away, or formally leaving a job/place.
出門 出國 走 離開
Image (圖示)
Explanation (解釋) Leaving home temporarily; you will return home later. Leaving your country temporarily; you will come back. Simply leaving a physical space or gathering. Formally leaving a place, job, or person; won't return for a long time.
Usage Context (使用情境) Leaving your home for work, school, or errands. Going abroad for travel, business, or studies. Daily life, leaving a party/gathering, leaving without detailed explanations. Leaving a country, resigning from a company, or breaking up.
Examples (例句)

2. 怎麼做 (How to do) vs 做什麼 (What to do)
💡 Explanation:
• 做什麼 asks "what action/thing" to do.
• 怎麼做 asks "how" or "in what method/way" to handle a situation.

我(ㄨㄛˇ)的(ㄉㄜ˙)老(ㄌㄠˇ)婆(ㄆㄛˊ)生(ㄕㄥ)氣(ㄑㄧˋ)了(ㄌㄜ˙)，我(ㄨㄛˇ)不(ㄅㄨˋ)知(ㄓ)道(ㄉㄠˋ)要(ㄧㄠˋ)怎(ㄗㄣˇ)麼(ㄇㄜ˙)做(ㄗㄨㄛˋ)，才(ㄘㄞˊ)會(ㄏㄨㄟˋ)讓(ㄖㄤˋ)她(ㄊㄚ)高(ㄍㄠ)興(ㄒㄧㄥˋ)。

3. Lesson 12 (第十二課)

1. 應該 (Probability Scale & Duty)
💡 Explanation: 應該 has two main uses:
1. Duty / Should: What ought to be done (e.g., You should bring an umbrella).
2. High Probability: Deducing a conclusion based on visible evidence.

Certainty Scale:
• 可能 (50% / May / Might) — Uncertain.
• 應該 (75% / Should / Likely) — High confidence based on evidence.
