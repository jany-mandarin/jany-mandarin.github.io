3. (人) + 人(ㄖㄣˊ)很(ㄏㄣˇ)好(ㄏㄠˇ) ("(Someone) is a nice person")

Explanation: 「(某人)人很好」means that person is kind / friendly. The opposite, 「人不好」, means that person isn't nice. 「(某人)是好人」means he/she is a good person — usually because they help others.

7. 來(ㄌㄞˊ) / 去(ㄑㄩˋ) (Come / Go)

Explanation: Whether to use 來 or 去 depends on distance from the speaker: moving toward the speaker's location is 來, moving away from it is 去. So if your wife's sister is heading to your house (where you, the speaker, are), you say she is 來 (coming), not 去.
來 (ㄌㄞˊ) 去 (ㄑㄩˋ)
