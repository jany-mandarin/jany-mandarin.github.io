一、暖身 (Warm-up)

Warm-up chat and vocabulary comparison.

1. 工作 vs 上班
🏷️ 工作 (gōngzuò) - Work / Job
詞性： 動詞、名詞 (Verb, Noun)
解釋： Job, work, tasks.

例句：
1. 我今天有很多工作。
2. 我的工作是老師。
3. 我明天要找新工作。 		說明 (Explanation):
• As a noun: Your job or profession (e.g., being a teacher).
• As tasks: Duties you need to do.
• '找新工作' means looking for a new job.
🏷️ 上班 (shàngbān) - Go to work
詞性： 動詞 (Verb)
解釋： Go to work, start work.

例句：
1. 我今天早上九點去上班。
2. 我現在在上班。
3. 我今天不用上班。 		說明 (Explanation):
• An action of going to your workplace or being on duty.
• '不用上班' means you don't have to work today (day off).

二、第九課 (Lesson 9)

Compound verbs, directions, and ordinal numbers.

1. 複合動詞 (Compound Verbs)
Verb + Result / Direction Complement (找 到、聽 懂、做 好)
🏷️ 複合動詞文法規則 (Grammar Rules)
肯定句： 主詞 + 動詞 + 結果 + 受詞 + 了
否定句： 主詞 + 沒有 + 動詞 + 結果 + 受詞 (不要加了)

常見動詞結果：
• 看見、看到 (see)
• 找到 (find successfully)
• 聽到、聽懂 (hear, understand)
• 做完、做好 (finish, prepare well) 		說明 (Explanation):
• In Chinese, adding a complement after a verb tells the result.
• Affirmative sentences end with '了'.
• Negative sentences use '沒有' and do NOT use '了'.
2. 互動練習 (Interactive Quiz)
Test your understanding. Click the options below!
1) 我找 ______ 我的錢包了。
I found my wallet.
2) 他沒有聽 ______ 老師的話。
He didn't hear / understand the teacher's words.
3) 我做 ______ 海南雞飯了，一起吃飯吧！
I have prepared Hainanese chicken rice, let's eat together!
3. 從 A 到 B (From A to B)
Used for time, location, or range.
从早上九点到下午五点我在上班。
I am at work from 9 AM to 5 PM.
从北京到上海要坐几个小时的飞机？
How many hours of flight is it from Beijing to Shanghai?
4. 第 x + 量詞 (Ordinal numbers)
Used to order books, lessons, or times.
這是我第一次跳舞。
This is my first time dancing.

三、第六課作業本

Review of frequency adverbs.

1. 時間副詞 (Frequency Adverbs)
🏷️ 常常 (chángcháng) - Often
例句： 我常常吃海南雞飯。 說明： Indicates high frequency (frequently).
2. 大夫 (dàifu) - Doctor
An affectionate term for a physician, especially TCM doctors.
我的爸爸是大夫。
My dad is a doctor.

四、第八課作業本

Polite refusal and reduplication.

1. 有點(兒) (yǒudiǎnr) - A little bit
A polite way to decline or express slight dissatisfaction in Chinese culture.
那件白的一點長。
That white piece of clothing is a bit long.
2. 重複字詞 (Reduplication of Verbs)
Repeating a verb makes the tone softer and casual.
我想找時間去看看老師。
I want to find some time to go visit the teacher.
