1. Warm-up 暖身

1. 工作 vs 上班

WordPart of speechMeaningExamples
工作{gōng zuò}Verb, Nounjob / occupation, task, the act of working
我今天有很多工作{wǒ jīn tiān yǒu hěn duō gōng zuò}。I have a lot of work today.
我的工作是老师{wǒ de gōng zuò shì lǎo shī}。My job is teacher.
我明天要去找新工作{wǒ míng tiān yào qù zhǎo xīn gōng zuò}。I'm going to look for a new job tomorrow.
上班{shàng bān}Verbgo to the workplace; start working
我今天早上九点去上班{wǒ jīn tiān zǎo shang jiǔ diǎn qù shàng bān}。I go to work at 9 a.m. today.
我现在在上班{wǒ xiàn zài zài shàng bān}。I'm at work now.
我今天不用上班{wǒ jīn tiān bú yòng shàng bān}。I don't have to work today.

2. Lesson 9 第九课

1. Compound verbs 复合动词 (Verb + Complement)

💡 Explanation: In Chinese, one sound can be many different characters, so people can easily misunderstand. We add a second character to make the word stronger and clearer.
Verbs: 看见{kàn jiàn}、看到{kàn dào}、找到{zhǎo dào}、听到{tīng dào}、听懂{tīng dǒng}、做完{zuò wán}、做好{zuò hǎo}
TypePatternExample
Positive 肯定句Subject + Verb + Object + 了{le}
我找到我的手机了{wǒ zhǎo dào wǒ de shǒu jī le}。I found my phone.
Negative 否定句Subject + 没(有) + Verb + Object
(do NOT add 了)
我没找到我的手机{wǒ méi zhǎo dào wǒ de shǒu jī}。I didn't find my phone.
Question 问句Positive sentence + 没有 at the end.
It is long, so in daily life people are lazy and shorten it.
你找到你的手机了没有{nǐ zhǎo dào nǐ de shǒu jī le méi yǒu}？Did you find your phone? (full form)
你找到你的手机了吗{nǐ zhǎo dào nǐ de shǒu jī le ma}？Did you find your phone? (easier, everyday)
WordMeaningExamples
看{kàn}Just look at / watch with your eyes.看书{kàn shū}、看电视{kàn diàn shì}、看电影{kàn diàn yǐng}read a book, watch TV, watch a movie
看见{kàn jiàn}Something "catches your eye", often by accident. Simple, objective, like a picture.
我在公司看见一只猫{wǒ zài gōng sī kàn jiàn yì zhī māo}。I saw a cat at the company.
看到{kàn dào}Emphasizes the result. (Many Chinese speakers prefer 看见.)
我看到一只猫在吃肉{wǒ kàn dào yì zhī māo zài chī ròu}。I saw a cat eating meat.
V. + 到 : emphasizes a result
吃到{chī dào}Suddenly eat something very tasty and want to share.
我吃到好吃的西安面了{wǒ chī dào hǎo chī de xī ān miàn le}。I ate delicious Xi'an noodles!
喝到{hē dào}Suddenly drink something very tasty and want to share.
我喝到好喝的红茶了{wǒ hē dào hǎo hē de hóng chá le}。I drank delicious black tea!
找到{zhǎo dào}Suddenly find something you were looking for.
我找到我的手机了{wǒ zhǎo dào wǒ de shǒu jī le}！！I found my phone!!
听到{tīng dào}You hear a sound that something makes, so we always add 到.
我听到他说的话了{wǒ tīng dào tā shuō de huà le}。I heard what he said.
V. + 懂 : understand after using a sense
看懂{kàn dǒng}Reading a book or watching a movie, and you want to understand.
你看懂这个电影吗{nǐ kàn dǒng zhè ge diàn yǐng ma}？我看不懂{wǒ kàn bu dǒng}。Did you understand this movie? I didn't understand.
听懂{tīng dǒng}Someone is talking, and you want to understand.
你听懂他说的话了吗{nǐ tīng dǒng tā shuō de huà le ma}？我听不懂{wǒ tīng bu dǒng}。Did you understand what he said? I didn't understand.
你听懂他说的中文了吗{nǐ tīng dǒng tā shuō de zhōng wén le ma}？我听懂了{wǒ tīng dǒng le}。Did you understand his Chinese? I understood.
WordMeaningExamples
V. + 好{hǎo}It is ready. You can start the next thing.
我做好饭了{wǒ zuò hǎo fàn le}，可以吃饭了{kě yǐ chī fàn le}。I finished cooking; we can eat now.
我写好作业了{wǒ xiě hǎo zuò yè le}。My homework is ready (done).
V. + 完{wán}The thing is finished. It is over.
我吃完饭了{wǒ chī wán fàn le}。I finished eating.
我做完第六课的考试题{wǒ zuò wán dì liù kè de kǎo shì tí}。I finished the Lesson 6 test questions.

2. 从 A 到 B (cóng A dào B)

💡 Explanation: Use 从 A 到 B for "from time/place A to time/place B".
从早上九点到下午五点我在上班{cóng zǎo shang jiǔ diǎn dào xià wǔ wǔ diǎn wǒ zài shàng bān}。From 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. I am at work.
从北京到上海要坐几个小时的飞机{cóng běi jīng dào shàng hǎi yào zuò jǐ ge xiǎo shí de fēi jī}？How many hours is the flight from Beijing to Shanghai?
从老人到小孩都喜欢吃苹果{cóng lǎo rén dào xiǎo hái dōu xǐ huan chī píng guǒ}。From old people to children, everyone likes apples.
Chinese culture respects older people, so we say 老人 (old people) first.
从小到大我都喜欢吃肉派{cóng xiǎo dào dà wǒ dōu xǐ huan chī ròu pài}。Since I was little, I have always liked meat pies.
Here 从小到大 is about yourself, from birth until now.
从下星期一开始上班{cóng xià xīng qī yī kāi shǐ shàng bān}。I start work from next Monday.

3. 第 + number + measure word

💡 Explanation: 第 makes a number into an order: first, second, third...
PatternExampleMeaning
第 + number + 本 + 书第三本书{dì sān běn shū}the third book
第 + number + 个 + 东西 / 人第五个人{dì wǔ ge rén}the fifth person
第 + number + 课第九课{dì jiǔ kè}Lesson 9
第 + number + 页第十页{dì shí yè}page 10
第 + number + 次第二次{dì èr cì}the second time
这是我第一次跳舞{zhè shì wǒ dì yī cì tiào wǔ}。This is my first time dancing.
她是我第一个女朋友{tā shì wǒ dì yī ge nǚ péng you}。She is my first girlfriend.
这是我的第一本书{zhè shì wǒ de dì yī běn shū}。This is my first book.

3. Workbook 6 第六课作业本

1. Time adverbs 时间副词

WordMeaningExample
常常{cháng cháng}often (a lot of times)
我常常吃海南鸡饭{wǒ cháng cháng chī hǎi nán jī fàn}。I often eat Hainanese chicken rice.
有时候{yǒu shí hou}sometimes (not always, not never)
我有时候去健身房{wǒ yǒu shí hou qù jiàn shēn fáng}。I sometimes go to the gym.
很少{hěn shǎo}rarely (almost never)
我很少吃水果{wǒ hěn shǎo chī shuǐ guǒ}。I rarely eat fruit.
他很少有时间休息{tā hěn shǎo yǒu shí jiān xiū xi}。He rarely has time to rest.

2. 大夫 (dài fu)

💡 Explanation: 大夫 is an old way to call a doctor. Some people still say it today, and it often means a doctor of Chinese medicine. Note: here 大 is read "dài", not "dà".
我的爸爸是大夫{wǒ de bà ba shì dài fu}。My father is a doctor.

4. Workbook 8 第八课作业本

1. 有点(儿) yǒu diǎn(r)

💡 Explanation: In Chinese culture, people often do not say "I don't like it" directly. They say 有点 + adjective to politely say no.
那件白的有点长{nà jiàn bái de yǒu diǎn cháng}。That white one is a little long.
This means: I like shorter ones. I don't like long ones.
这件黑的有点贵{zhè jiàn hēi de yǒu diǎn guì}。This black one is a little expensive.
This means: it is too expensive, I don't like it, I don't want to buy it, but we don't say it so directly.

2. Repeated words 重复字词

💡 Explanation: Chinese likes to say a verb twice. It makes the action lighter and softer ("a little"), and it also sounds cute.
我想找时间去看看老师{wǒ xiǎng zhǎo shí jiān qù kàn kan lǎo shī}。I want to find time to go see my teacher.
我想去运动运动{wǒ xiǎng qù yùn dòng yùn dong}。I want to go exercise a bit.
