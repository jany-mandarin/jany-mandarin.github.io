一、暖身 (Warm-up)

Warm-up chat and vocabulary comparison.

1. 工作 vs 上班
🏷️ 工(gōng)作(zuò) (Work / Job)
詞性：動詞、名詞
意思：Profession, tasks, labor itself.

例句：
1. 我wǒ今tiān天有hěn很多gōng工zuò作。
2. 我de的gōng工zuò作是lǎo老shī師。
3. 我míng明tiān天要zhǎo找新gōng工zuò作。		 說明 (Explanation in English):
• As a noun, it means your profession or job (e.g., being a teacher).
• As a verb or noun, it refers to tasks and duties you need to complete.
• When looking for employment, you search for a 'new job' (新工作).
🏷️ 上(shàng)班(bān) (Go to work / Be on duty)
詞性：動詞
意思：To go to the company, start working status.

例句：
1. 我wǒ今tiān天早shàng上九diǎn點去shàng上bān班。
2. 我xiàn現在zài在shàng上bān班。
3. 我jīn今tiān天不yòng用shàng上bān班。		 說明 (Explanation in English):
• An action verb describing the daily routine of heading to your workplace or being on duty.
• '不用上班' means you don't have to work today (day off).

二、第九課 (Lesson 9)

Compound verbs, directions, and ordinal numbers.

1. 複合動詞 (Compound Verbs: Verb + Result/Direction Complement)
Explanation: In Chinese, single-syllable verbs can be ambiguous. Adding a complement word emphasizes the result, completion, or sensory capture.
肯定句：主zhǔ詞 + 動dòng詞 + 受shòu詞 + 了
Affirmative: Subject + Verb + Object + 了
否定句：主zhǔ詞 + 沒méi有 + 動dòng詞 + 受shòu詞 (Note: 不要加了)
Negative: Subject + 没(有) + Verb + Object (Do NOT add 了)
2. 互動練習 (Interactive Quiz)
Test your understanding of compound verbs. Click the options below!
1) 我找 ______ 我的錢包了。
I found my wallet.
2) 他沒有聽 ______ 老師的話。
He didn't hear / understand the teacher's words.
3) 我做 ______ 海南雞飯了，一起吃飯吧！
I have prepared Hainanese chicken rice, let's eat together!
3. 從 A 到 B (From A to B)
Explanation: Used for time, location, or progressive range.
从早上九点到下午五点我在上班。
I am at work from 9 AM to 5 PM.
从北京到上海要坐几个小时的飞机？
How many hours of flight is it from Beijing to Shanghai?
4. 第 x + 量詞 (Ordinal numbers + Measure words)
Explanation: Used to order books, people, lessons, pages, or frequencies.
這是我第一次跳舞。
This is my first time dancing.

三、第六課作業本

Review of frequency adverbs and traditional medical terms.

1. 時間副詞 (Frequency Adverbs)
🏷️ 常(cháng)常(cháng) (Often)
例句：
我常常吃海南雞飯。		 說明 (Explanation in English):
• Indicates high frequency of an action (frequently / often).
2. 大夫 (dài fu - Doctor)
Explanation: An ancient Chinese term for physicians. Today, people still use it affectionately, particularly for Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) doctors.
我的爸爸是大夫。
My dad is a doctor.

四、第八課作業本

Polite refusal and reduplication of verbs.

1. 有點(兒) (yǒu diǎn(r) - A little bit)
Explanation: In Chinese culture, people often avoid direct blunt refusals. Saying 有点 + adjective is a polite way to decline or express slight dissatisfaction.
那件白的一點長。
That white piece of clothing is a bit long.
2. 重複字詞 (Reduplication of Verbs)
Explanation: Repeating a verb twice makes the tone softer, casual, and friendly, implying doing something briefly or recreationally.
我想找時間去看看老師。
I want to find some time to go visit the teacher.
